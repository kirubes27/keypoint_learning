## What's established (verified this session, with evidence)

1. **Operator emergence is robust** — A ≈ R(+6°) in all 324 (‖A−R(6°)‖ median 0.03). BUT Task 80 has inv=cyc=0.5 on, so its rotation is *assisted*. **For the clean "emerges from prediction alone" claim, cite the `inv=cyc=0` cells** (36 of them; they also emerge).
2. **No representation-vs-dynamics conflict under the collapse-neutral metric.** Spearman(separation, identity-normalized rollout AUC) = −0.035. The old conflict was the `k10/k1` artifact.
3. **`variance-normalized` AUC rewards collapse** (top-5 configs by it are fully collapsed, dup=1.0, sep≈1e-7). Use it as a **diagnostic only, never a winner metric**. (identity-norm = model_mse/motion = collapse-neutral; variance-norm = model_mse/state-spread = collapse-favoring.)
4. **Jitter is an architectural floor, NOT worse than before.** Measured ~1 heatmap cell in both old yaw (0.96 cells) and new roll (1.11 cells). In *normalized coords* it's actually smaller now (0.035 vs 0.062, finer 64×64 vs old 32×32 heatmap) and smaller relative to motion (jitter/motion 0.59 vs 1.20). It only *seems* new because roll gives a clean derotation reference and we now measure per-frame stability. Present to Angela as "pre-existing floor we exposed, not a regression."
5. **Duplicates: good configs don't persistently collapse.** Task 80 (proper trajectory-wide, diagonal excluded): no pair close >50% of frames; min-NN≈0 is *transient orbit-crossings*, not collapse. The earlier "all 324 have duplicates" was overstated (frame-88=176° was a worst-case single-frame snapshot). `disp=0.1+ent=0.01` reduces severe collapse to 1–2 persistent pairs/run but never zero. Persistent collapse lives in the `disp=0` configs.
6. **Entropy hypothesis FALSIFIED.** Higher `lambda_ent` → sharper heatmaps but *more* sliding/collapse (clean_kp_frac: ent 0.01→0.5 drives 0.17→0.00). Low entropy (0.01) is best.
7. **Forward pass is deterministic; BatchNorm eval/train negligible.** No stochastic/BN bug. (Does NOT prove training reproducibility — that needs the multi-seed audit.)

## The two core problems + how to solve them

### A. Duplicates / collapse
Root cause (verified): Gaussian dispersion `exp(−d²/σ²)` has vanishing gradient as d→0; and ANY isotropic pairwise loss has *undefined direction* at exact coincidence (topological — verified hinge gives [0,0] at d=0). So mostly a **prevention** problem (good configs don't collapse).
Fix (layered): (1) channel-specific init/embeddings (symmetry-break so channels can't become exact copies); (2) min-distance hinge `Σ max(0,m−‖p_i−p_j‖)²` (strong force *before* merge — verified 0.20 vs Gaussian 2e-5 at d=1e-7); (3) heatmap-overlap penalty `Σ⟨H_i,H_j⟩`; (4) MUST pair with on-object pressure (else separation flees to background). No single loss makes duplicates impossible.
Papers: KeypointNet (Suwajanakorn 2018, separation+silhouette+collapse warning); B-KinD (Sun 2022, equivariance→center-collapse needs separation — = our dead KP8); Lorenz 2019 (separation→grid-artifact caveat). **Verify exact eq/page numbers before citing.**

### B. Object-relative stability (sliding)
Decompose first: (1) ~1-cell **grid jitter** → resolution, not a loss; (2) **slow sliding** → descriptor; (3) **peak-switching** (KP5/7 on symmetric hammer ends) → contrastive descriptor.
- **Resolution fix (jitter):** make the heatmap the soft-argmax reads from 128×128 (reduce one stride-2 / add upsample before heatmap head — NOT padding, NOT the 512 image). Cell 0.032→0.016, jitter ~halves. Test 64 vs 128, backbone fixed.
- **Contrastive descriptor consistency (sliding+switching, = refined version of Angela's local-patch idea):** `d_i(t)≈d_i(t+1)` AND `d_i(t)≉d_j(t+1)` (negatives). Use a descriptor (normalized gradients/learned feats), NOT raw pixels (raw favors bland regions + the patch rotates 6° under roll). Avoid descriptor-collapse (stop-gradient / frozen / negatives). Contrastive version also discourages duplicates.
- Pairwise rigidity = roll-only ablation (preserves distances but doesn't fix duplicates/switching, fails under yaw/pitch).
Papers: UCN (Choy 2016, contrastive correspondence); Transporter (Kulkarni 2019, feature-transport fallback).
**Do NOT impose `K(x_{θ+6})=R₆K(x_θ)`** — that hands the model the rotation and changes the scientific question.
