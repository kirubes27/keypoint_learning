*Scope: ONLY the unsupervised keypoint discovery / operator-simplicity thread. The parallel mechanistic-interpretability work (LLM uncertainty, ICML workshop, MATS) is deliberately excluded. ## TL;DR
- **The empty cell is real but narrow.** No 2023–2026 paper occupies the exact triad — unsupervised soft-argmax CNN keypoint chart + one shared global 2×2+bias operator + strictly reconstruction-free, label-free temporal prediction — but four neighbors (Miyato/MSP NeurIPS 2022, Ruiz-Morales/Koopman-JEPA AAAI-2026, Koyama/NFT ICLR 2024, and Hongbo Wang's June-2026 equivariant-JEPA preprint) plus Keurti's HAE crowd the framing and must be cited-and-distinguished. Novelty rests on the *spatial keypoint chart* + *rigid 2×2 operator* + *no decoder*, **not** on the generic "linear predictor learned by prediction" insight, which is now actively discussed in the JEPA literature.
- **The load-bearing experiment is the generic-20D-latent ablation, and it must be finished before Paper 1 is submittable.** The operator-recovery result (median ‖A−R(6°)‖ ≈ 0.03 on clean cells) is solid but too small for a top-tier main track alone; the honest-negative representation result (231/324 collapse; best clean_kp_frac = 0.5) is the actual scientific contribution and needs the irrep-selection ablation, collapse fixes, static+identity exclusion, and multi-object generalization to reach NeurIPS/ICLR/CVPR weight.
- **Realistic arc: 4 papers over 3.5 years.** Paper 1 (irrep-selection / "predictability is not enough") → ICLR 2027 (full-paper deadline **Sep 24, 2026**; abstract Sep 19) if the ablation lands, else NeurIPS 2027 (~May 2027); Paper 2 (SO(3)/compositionality) → NeurIPS 2027 or CVPR 2028; Paper 3 (branch: 3D-depth vs non-rigid) → CVPR/ICCV 2028; capstone (non-rigid faces + semantic keypoints) → cognitive-science flagship (PNAS / Nature Human Behaviour) in year 4. **NeurIPS 2026 (full paper May 6, 2026) has already passed** and is not reachable.

## Key Findings

**1. Metadata verification (both flagged refs confirmed correct).**
- **Hedlin 2024 — arXiv:2312.00065 is CORRECT.** "Unsupervised Keypoints from Pretrained Diffusion Models," Eric Hedlin, Gopal Sharma, Shweta Mahajan, Xingzhe He, Hossam Isack, Abhishek Kar, Helge Rhodin, Andrea Tagliasacchi, Kwang Moo Yi (UBC / Vector / Google Research / Google DeepMind / SFU / Toronto). - **Ruiz-Morales 2026 — arXiv:2511.09783 is CORRECT and now has a venue.** "Koopman Invariants as Drivers of Emergent Time-Series Clustering in Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures," Pablo Ruiz-Morales, Dries Vanoost, Davy Pissoort, Mathias Verbeke (DTAI/ESAT-WaveCore, M-Group, KU Leuven / Flanders Make@KU Leuven, Bruges). arXiv v1 timestamped **12 Nov 2025 22:33:56 UTC**; PDF header states it is "accepted at the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence 2026" (Proc. AAAI vol. 40, OJS article 39708, pp. 25176–25183). Verbatim from the paper: *"We prove that an idealized JEPA loss is minimized when the encoder represents the system's regime indicator functions, which are Koopman eigenfunctions… constraining the JEPA's linear predictor to be a near-identity operator is the key inductive bias that forces the encoder to learn these invariants… critical for selecting this interpretable solution from a class of mathematically equivalent but entangled optima."* This is the single most theoretically-relevant same-mechanism paper on the list.

**2. A critical reference is MISSING from the 34.** **Miyato, Koyama & Fukumizu, "Unsupervised Learning of Equivariant Structure from Sequences," arXiv:2210.05972, NeurIPS 2022 (MSP).** This is the *closest conceptual ancestor* to the irrep-selection claim: it learns latent linear transition operators from future prediction over sequences and recovers block-diagonal (irrep-type) structure via simultaneous block-diagonalization. It shares authors with Koyama/NFT. **Add it to positioning_refs.csv and treat it as Tier 1** — a reviewer who knows the NFT lineage will know MSP, and its existence is the biggest single reason the irrep framing is not fully novel. Crucial distinguisher: MSP uses an encoder–**decoder** trained to predict future *observations* (it reconstructs); this project is reconstruction-free and lives in an on-object soft-argmax keypoint chart pinned to the k=1 fundamental.

**3. The closest single artifact to the project's own naming is a June-2026 preprint.** **Hongbo Wang, "Exact equivariance, kept through training, buys zero-shot generalisation across the symmetry group," arXiv:2606.03003 (Stony Brook, single author).** It builds a JEPA latent world model with an equivariant encoder + equivariant predictor and shows one-step prediction error is exactly invariant across the group orbit. **It does NOT scoop:** the group and orthogonal representation ρ(g) are hardcoded via Vector-Neuron/e3nn intertwiner parametrization (not a freely-learned 2×2 + bias), there is no unsupervised soft-argmax keypoint chart, and it uses VICReg anti-collapse plus an explicit pixel-coordinate planning branch. But it is a topically adjacent, laptop-scale manuscript that must be cited and distinguished — and is a canary that others are circling this conceptual space.

**4. The empty-cell verdict: reasonably safe, with named crowding.** Object-centric/slot dynamics models (SlotFormer, SOLD arXiv:2410.08822, SlotContrast arXiv:2412.14295, Latent Particle World Models arXiv:2603.04553, and 2026 successors) universally rely on reconstruction (pixel or feature) and nonlinear Transformer/GNN dynamics — none use a single shared linear operator across slots learned by prediction alone. Equivariant-keypoint work (USEEK arXiv:2209.13864, Steerers arXiv:2312.02152, oriented-keypoint detectors) is either supervised-by-known-warp or descriptor-matching, not prediction-only operator learning. The generic insight "linear/near-identity predictor learned by prediction ⇒ structured latents" is now actively discussed in the JEPA literature (Ruiz-Morales; multiple 2025–2026 reviews), so the project must **not** claim that as novel; foreground the keypoint-chart + rigid-operator + no-decoder specifics.

## Deliverable 3: Fresh Literature Scan (2024–2026) & Empty-Cell Assessment

**Threats searched and verdicts:**
- **JEPA / linear-predictability line (highest-velocity threat).** Ruiz-Morales AAAI-2026 (near-identity linear predictor ⇒ Koopman-invariant latents) is the same mechanism on time-series latents. seq-JEPA (arXiv:2505.03176, v3 Jan 2026) learns architecturally-segregated equivariant/invariant representations by action-conditioned prediction — action-conditioned and abstract, not a shared linear operator on keypoints. V-JEPA 2 / 2.1 (arXiv:2506.09985; 2603.14482) explicitly note JEPA latents are *not* spatially structured, which actually supports the project's differentiation. **Verdict: neighbors, not scoops — but this is where a scoop would come from.**
- **Object-centric / slot dynamics (2024–2026): SOLD (2410.08822), SlotContrast (2412.14295), Latent Particle World Models (2603.04553)** and 2026 successors all use reconstruction (pixel or feature) and nonlinear Transformer/GNN dynamics. **None use a single shared linear operator across slots by prediction alone.** OCVP-style weight-sharing across slots is a structural cousin worth acknowledging.
- **Equivariant keypoints: Steerers (2312.02152), oriented-keypoint detectors (Lee 2022/2023), USEEK (2209.13864)** are descriptor-matching or supervised-by-known-warp; not prediction-only operator learning. **No threat.**
- **Irrep-selection / harmonic degeneracy in prediction-trained reps:** beyond MSP/NFT, no 2025–2026 paper frames this for a *reconstruction-free* equivariant representation. **This sub-cell is open**, but the phenomenon is already discussed under reconstruction (MSP/NFT) — novelty hinges on the no-decoder + keypoint specifics.
